# LAUNCH STRATEGY

## Launch Objective

Operio aims to raise an initial $50–100k through a Web3 launchpad, focusing on platforms that support early-stage B2B infrastructure projects. The funds will be used to finalize our MVP, onboard the first batch of users, and begin testing our token model within a real ERP environment.

## Launchpad Selection Criteria

We are currently evaluating mid-sized launchpads that meet the following criteria:

- Have an active and qualified investor base  
- Are looking to list early-stage B2B/infra projects  
- Are open to hybrid models (Web2 SaaS + Web3 token utility)  
- Provide guidance without requiring complex token vesting lock-ins  

Our approach avoids overly saturated platforms and instead focuses on high-signal communities or newer launchpads with solid internal backers.

## Web3 Angle and Differentiation

Operio introduces Web3 dynamics into the traditionally closed ERP space. By allowing token-based governance, staking-based access, and future integrations for crypto-based invoicing and smart contract orchestration, we bring decentralization and automation into real operational workflows.

Unlike other projects that introduce tokens purely for speculation, Operio’s utility token will serve to:

- Unlock modules and premium features  
- Govern roadmap priorities  
- Power decentralized incentives for development and adoption  
- Enable crypto-native billing and payments

## Network Effect and Token Growth Strategy

Our go-to-market will prioritize:

- Strategic partnerships with emerging DAOs and B2B Web3 collectives  
- Referral programs powered by smart contracts  
- On-chain user reward systems  
- Open-source SDK to allow 3rd party integrations using the Operio token
