## Executive Summary

**Operio** is the world’s first **AGI-native ERP platform**, designed to bridge the gap between enterprise-grade operations and open agent-based intelligence. Built for builders, Operio empowers both companies and individual creators to design, train, and deploy autonomous business agents redefining how work gets done.

While legacy ERPs like SAP, Oracle, or Odoo require manual setup, long integrations, and steep learning curves, Operio offers a modular, intelligent, and composable alternative powered by a real-time orchestrator and vertical cognitive agents. Whether you're a fast-scaling startup or a Web3-native collective, Operio adapts to how you actually work.

At the core of this ecosystem lies the **$OPXR Token**, enabling a permissionless marketplace where developers, ops specialists, and data contributors can train and sell agents, stake on automation outcomes, and co-own the evolution of the platform contributing in shaping the future of enterprise systems, autonomous agents and enabling the migration from human workforce to AI. Operio gives back control to its community: companies can stake $OPXR to unlock premium modules, while creators can monetize automations through the marketplace.

We’re not building just another ERP.  
We’re replacing the outdated corporate stack with a smarter, adaptive, and transparent operating system built by people, powered by AGI.
