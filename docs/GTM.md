# GO-TO-MARKET & COMMUNITY STRATEGY

## Target Audience

Operio is designed for forward-thinking founders, COOs, and operations teams in Web3 and high-growth SaaS companies. We target:

- Agile B2B teams that need modular, AI-native ERP  
- Web3-native startups looking to integrate crypto workflows  
- Communities and DAOs building internal operational stacks  

This hybrid approach allows us to enter both the traditional SaaS market and the decentralized ecosystem with a real use case.

## Early Traction Strategy

We plan to onboard our first 10 design partners through:

- Direct outreach to SaaS/Web3 founders  
- Invitations via X (Twitter), Telegram, and selected Web3 communities  
- Offering heavily discounted access to early adopters  
- Giving feedback channels and visibility into product decisions  

These users will help validate the MVP, shape features, and build initial product-market fit.

## Community Growth Strategy

Our community strategy focuses on depth before scale. Key actions:

- Create a gated Telegram/Discord for builders, partners, and investors  
- Publish weekly dev logs and roadmap updates via GitHub & Mirror  
- Hold AMA sessions and Twitter Spaces pre-launch  
- Use Zealy or Galxe for reward-based onboarding and Web3 engagement  
- Launch a Notion-style public roadmap where token holders can vote and comment  

## Narrative and Positioning

We position Operio not just as an ERP, but as:

> “The AGI-native OS for the future of work.”

This narrative allows us to differentiate from legacy SaaS and no-code platforms, while appealing to the experimental, forward-looking mindset of Web3.

## Content & Thought Leadership

- Publish cross-posted content on Mirror, Paragraph, and Medium  
- Use AI to generate educational micro-content (threads, explainers, case studies)  
- Collaborate with design partners for co-marketing launches  
- Position the founder as a thought leader on AI, operations, and token-driven SaaS
