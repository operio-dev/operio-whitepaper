# Revenue Model

Operio’s monetization strategy is designed not just to generate recurring revenue, but to align platform value with customer outcomes. By blending predictable subscription tiers with usage-based components and a growing ecosystem, Operio can serve startups and scaleups while capturing enterprise demand.

### Subscription Plans
- **Tiered SaaS Pricing:** Customers choose from multiple subscription levels based on number of users, modules activated, and usage volume.
- **Monthly & Annual Billing:** Flexible billing cycles with discounts for annual commitments.

*Free Starter Tier: A limited-access plan designed for startups and solo operators to test core features with no commitment, fostering early traction*.

### Usage-Based Fees
- **Agent API Calls:** Pricing scales with the number of AI agent interactions, reflecting computational costs and value delivered. Basic plans include a generous quota of agent calls per month to ensure usability and adoption before incremental costs apply.
- **Add-On Services:** Premium features such as advanced analytics, integrations, and custom workflows are available as paid add-ons.

### Enterprise Licensing
- **Custom Contracts:** For large organizations requiring bespoke features, dedicated support, and on-premises deployment options.

### Marketplace Revenue
- **Third-Party Extensions:** Revenue share from marketplace apps and plugins developed by partners.
Partners can build, monetize, and distribute apps on the Operio Marketplace, with a transparent rev-share model and API documentation support. Community-created modules can expand Operio’s capabilities beyond the core roadmap.

---

This multi-layered approach allows Operio to cater to startups, SMEs, and large enterprises, aligning revenue with delivered value and growth potential.
