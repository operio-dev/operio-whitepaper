# Revenue Model

Operio's monetization strategy represents a paradigm shift from traditional ERP economics. Where legacy vendors extract value through complexity and lock-in, we create compounding returns by amplifying human productivity with AGI. Our model doesn't just scale with customer growth—it scales with customer success.

## The AGI Revenue Thesis

In the pre-AGI era, software value was linear: more users, more revenue. Operio operates on exponential economics. As our AI agents learn and optimize business processes, the value delivered grows non-linearly while marginal costs approach zero. This creates unprecedented unit economics and expansion potential.

## Revenue Architecture

### Subscription Foundation: Predictable Growth Engine

**Three-Tier SaaS Model** designed around AI sophistication, not seat count:

- **Starter ($49/user/month):** Core ERP modules with foundational AI agents. Perfect for startups and SMEs testing AGI-enhanced workflows.
- **Professional ($149/user/month):** Advanced AI orchestration, custom automation builder, API access, and predictive business intelligence.
- **Enterprise ($299/user/month):** Full AGI suite with white-label deployment, custom model training, and dedicated infrastructure.

**Billing Intelligence:** Monthly for cash flow flexibility, annual contracts with 25% savings. Our enterprise customers average 18-month commitment cycles with 95% renewal rates.

**Strategic Free Tier—"Founder Edition":** Complete platform access for teams under 3 users. Beta metrics show 34% conversion to paid within 120 days, with average customer lifetime value of $47K.

### Usage-Based Value Capture: The AGI Multiplier

**AI Agent Consumption Model:** Beyond base allocations, customers pay $0.015 per agent interaction. This reflects true computational cost while capturing exponential value delivery.

**Generous Base Quotas:**
- Starter: 2,500 interactions/month
- Professional: 25,000 interactions/month
- Enterprise: Unlimited (fair use policy)

**Power users represent 12% of our customer base but generate 41% of usage revenue—classic platform economics.**

**Premium Add-On Ecosystem:**
- Advanced Forecasting AI: $299/month (enables 15-30% inventory optimization)
- Real-Time Business Intelligence: $199/month (reduces decision latency by 85%)
- Custom Integration Builder: $149/month (replaces $50K+ custom development)
- Multi-Language AI: $99/month (instant global deployment)

### Enterprise Revenue Scale: The Competitive Moat

**Custom Deployment Contracts** starting at $150K annually:
- Private cloud or on-premises deployment with dedicated infrastructure
- Custom AI model training on proprietary business data and processes
- White-label platform licensing with full co-branding capabilities
- Dedicated Customer Success Manager with guaranteed response SLAs
- Advanced compliance (SOC2, ISO27001, GDPR, CCPA, industry-specific standards)

**Professional Services Revenue:** Implementation, training, and custom development typically represent 20-35% of initial contract value. 90% gross margins with high strategic value.

### Marketplace Ecosystem: Network Effects at Scale

**Partner Revenue Share:** 30% commission on third-party applications and integrations. Our API-first architecture enables:
- Industry-specific modules (manufacturing, retail, healthcare)
- Advanced integrations with existing enterprise tools
- Vertical-specific AI agents and workflows

**Marketplace Launch Strategy:** Q3 2025 with 15+ launch partners already committed. Target: 200+ applications by end of 2026.
