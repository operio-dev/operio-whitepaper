# Operio Tokenomics

## Total Token Supply  
- Total fixed supply: 500,000,000 (500 million) tokens  
- No inflation, no minting after initial issuance

### Token Allocation (Revised)

| Category                     | Allocation | Notes |
|-----------------------------|------------|-------|
| Team & Founders             | 18%        | 24 months vesting, 6-month cliff |
| Private Sale / Seed         | 15%        | 12 months vesting, 3-month cliff |
| Public Sale / IDO           | 10%        | Unlocked at TGE, optional 3-month linear vesting |
| Ecosystem & Community       | 23%        | Rewards, staking, contributor incentives |
| DAO Treasury                | 5%         | Governance fund for proposals and votes |
| Advisors & Contributors     | 5%         | 12 months vesting, 3-month cliff |
| Marketing & Growth          | 10%        | Campaigns, ambassadors, KOLs |
| Liquidity Provision         | 5%         | Locked liquidity on DEX/CEX pools |
| Operational Reserve         | 9%         | Emergency buffer, unexpected costs |

## Vesting & Lock-up  
- Team & Founders: 24 months vesting with 6-month cliff  
- Private Sale Investors: 12 months vesting with 3-month cliff  
- Advisors: 12 months vesting with 3-month cliff  
- Public Sale tokens unlocked at TGE (Token Generation Event) with possible linear vesting over 3 months

  ### Liquidity Provision Strategy

Operio will allocate 5% of the total supply to initial DEX and CEX liquidity pools. Liquidity will be locked for a minimum of 12 months post-TGE to ensure price stability and build trust with early adopters. Additional liquidity may be injected over time depending on trading volume and platform growth.

## Token Utility  
- Payment for Operio platform subscriptions and services  
- Access to premium features and modules within Operio  
- Governance rights via DAO voting mechanisms  
- Incentives and rewards for early adopters, community contributors, and partners

- **Staking-as-Access**: Users can stake $OPR tokens to unlock access to premium AI agents and enterprise modules.
- **Burn Mechanism**: A percentage of agent upgrade fees and workflow customizations paid in $OPR may be burned, introducing a deflationary dynamic.
- **DAO Governance**: $OPR holders can vote on development priorities, integrations, and token allocation proposals via Operio DAO.
- **Reputation Layer**: Verified stakers or contributors may receive reputation scores, boosting their visibility in the Operio Marketplace.

## Notes  
- No additional tokens will be minted post-launch to avoid dilution  
- Token release schedules are designed to maintain price stability and long-term project commitment  
- Allocations can be adjusted based on community and investor feedback during the funding process


### Tokenomics Rationale

The $OPR token is designed with a fixed supply of 500 million and no future inflation, ensuring long-term scarcity and alignment. The distribution is engineered to balance founder and investor incentives with strong ecosystem participation and community empowerment.

Long-term vesting schedules prevent early dumps, while DAO governance and staking mechanisms give $OPR real functional and strategic value. Community incentives, contributor rewards, and burn-based utility drive continued engagement and value creation.

This structure positions Operio for sustainable growth, healthy market dynamics, and transparent stakeholder alignment.

### Token Launch Roadmap (Preliminary)

- **Q3 2025**: Finalize smart contracts, security audit
- **Q4 2025**: Private Sale, DAO setup, community onboarding
- **Q1 2026**: IDO / Public Sale + Liquidity lock (DEX/CEX)
- **Q2 2026**: Launch staking program and governance voting
- **Q3 2026**: DAO treasury activation, marketplace integration

### Final Notes

- No additional tokens will ever be minted, protecting holders from dilution.
- Vesting and release schedules are designed to ensure long-term alignment and discourage speculative behavior.
- DAO mechanisms will gradually decentralize governance over treasury and strategic decisions.
- Allocation percentages may be fine-tuned based on community and early investor feedback prior to TGE.
