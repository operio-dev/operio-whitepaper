Unlike closed venture rounds that concentrate ownership among institutional investors, our IDO approach enables broad community participation, creating thousands of stakeholders who are invested in platform success and motivated to drive adoption through their networks.

**IDO Structure and Mechanics:**

Our token allocation strategy balances community growth with long-term value creation. The IDO will offer 15% of total token supply (150 million $OPER tokens) at €0.015 per token, creating €2.25M in community funding. This pricing reflects current market conditions while providing attractive upside potential for early community members who recognize the AGI-native enterprise opportunity.

This structure incentivizes long-term community engagement while providing immediate utility for platform interaction.
