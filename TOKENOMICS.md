# Operio Tokenomics

## Total Token Supply  
- Total fixed supply: 500,000,000 (500 million) tokens  
- No inflation, no minting after initial issuance

## Token Allocation  
- Team & Founders: 20% (100 million)  
- Private Sale / Seed Investors: 15% (75 million)  
- Public Sale / Launchpad (IDO): 10% (50 million)  
- Ecosystem & Community Incentives: 20% (100 million)  
- Operational Reserve & Partnerships: 20% (100 million)  
- Advisors & Contributors: 5% (25 million)  
- Marketing & Growth: 10% (50 million)  

## Vesting & Lock-up  
- Team & Founders: 24 months vesting with 6-month cliff  
- Private Sale Investors: 12 months vesting with 3-month cliff  
- Advisors: 12 months vesting with 3-month cliff  
- Public Sale tokens unlocked at TGE (Token Generation Event) with possible linear vesting over 3 months

## Token Utility  
- Payment for Operio platform subscriptions and services  
- Access to premium features and modules within Operio  
- Governance rights via DAO voting mechanisms  
- Incentives and rewards for early adopters, community contributors, and partners  

## Notes  
- No additional tokens will be minted post-launch to avoid dilution  
- Token release schedules are designed to maintain price stability and long-term project commitment  
- Allocations can be adjusted based on community and investor feedback during the funding process


## Tokenomics Overview and Rationale

The Operio tokenomics model has been carefully designed to balance sustainable growth, fair distribution, and long-term project alignment. With a fixed supply of 500 million tokens, the structure ensures scarcity and value retention while avoiding inflation and dilution.

Allocations are split strategically to incentivize the founding team, reward early investors, and fuel community engagement. Vesting periods and cliffs protect the token economy from sudden dumps, aligning incentives for all stakeholders to contribute actively over time.

A significant portion is reserved for ecosystem development and partnerships, enabling Operio to build a thriving network and accelerate adoption. The token’s utility extends beyond governance, serving as a payment method within the platform and as a mechanism to reward valuable contributions from users and partners.

This balanced approach positions Operio for scalable growth while maintaining transparency and investor confidence.
